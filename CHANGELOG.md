# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-13

- Expanded to 252 catalog records, 44 source editions, 43 searchable works and 20,991 segments.
- Added thirteen biblical books, three Upanishads, Enoch, the Poetic Edda, and clearly labeled historical studies of Gilgamesh and the Egyptian Book of the Dead.
- Added three cited editorial guides and two textual-tension cases with alternative interpretations and scope filtering.
- Added source-section preservation checks and corrected partial-source availability labels.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-13

- Created the local Atlas application, responsive light/dark interface and all main research routes.
- Added 251 metadata records, 24 complete digitized editions / 23 works and 10,114 verbatim source segments.
- Added PostgreSQL schema, embedded database, source retrieval, provider abstraction, evidence labels and citation views.
- Added text/translation comparison, map/time controls, thematic relationships, canon browsing and museum object attribution.
- Added persistent collections, notes, exports and Windows startup launcher.
- Added ingestion tools, source/neutrality/copyright policies and automated verification.
- Corrected edition associations and the Meditations translator attribution to Meric Casaubon during integrity checks.
