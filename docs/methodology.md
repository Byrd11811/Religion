# Methodology

Read a source before comparing it. The primary work, its historical transmission, its English translator, and any editorial introduction are separate layers. Atlas preserves the complete body of each selected digitized edition and labels its application-generated segment locators. Every passage links back to its edition.

Local retrieval finds words and related concepts, consults PostgreSQL full-text search, and selects a diverse set of works. This ranking is a reading aid. The optional vector provider adds semantic candidates only when matching-model vectors already exist. Optional synthesis is explicitly labeled and requires citations and a second audit. Neither model checks nor a search score certify historical truth.

Comparison tables retain tradition, date range, region, language, canon context and uncertainty. Similarities, differences and historical transmission must be assessed independently. D and E labels remain visible on provisional or exploratory relationships. Unknown details are not completed by inference from a regional grouping.

The atlas favors neither a religion nor a critique of religion. It supports questions and source inspection rather than a single score of correctness. See the application’s About / Methodology page for the reader-facing explanation and coverage notes.
