## Counting rules

- **works:** Distinct work IDs; legacy texts and derived works are the same works, not additive. Source: `data/seed/texts.json`.
- **traditions:** Distinct navigation grouping IDs; not reviewed community taxonomy. Source: `data/seed/traditions.json`.
- **editions:** Distinct edition IDs. Source: `data/seed/editions.json`.
- **searchableWorks:** Distinct works with nonempty passages and edition source, license and hash metadata; not certification of primary content or license law. Source: `data/seed/editions.json + data/seed/passages.json`.
- **completeEditionWorks:** Distinct populated works with edition complete flag; historical study vs named work remains source-specific. Source: `data/seed/editions.json`.
- **metadataOnly:** No edition association, regardless of decorative metadata. Source: `data/seed/texts.json + data/seed/editions.json`.
- **passages:** Distinct segment IDs; not all segments are primary text. Source: `data/seed/passages.json`.
- **primaryPassages:** Only explicit contentType labels count; edition wording does not establish primary status. Source: `data/seed/passages.json`.
- **editorialPassages:** Only explicit contentType labels count; edition wording does not establish primary status. Source: `data/seed/passages.json`.
- **unclassifiedPassages:** Segments not explicitly labeled primary or editorial. Source: `data/seed/passages.json`.
- **witnesses:** Distinct imported and starter witness IDs, independent of works. Source: `packages/shared/archive.ts + data/seed/archive-additions.json`.
- **artifacts:** Distinct physical object IDs; not additional works or first-class artwork catalog coverage. Source: `packages/shared/archive.ts + data/seed/archive-additions.json`.
- **images:** Distinct image IDs; externally hosted references, not downloaded binaries or current URL health certification. Source: `packages/shared/archive.ts + data/seed/archive-additions.json`.
- **rightsAttributedImages:** Recorded public-domain assertion and attribution; not fresh legal review. Source: `packages/shared/archive.ts + data/seed/archive-additions.json`.
- **relationships:** Distinct explicit edges; do not infer relationships from similarity. Source: `data/seed/relationships.json`.
- **datedWorks:** Non-null start date; includes provisional dates. Source: `data/seed/texts.json`.
- **undatedWorks:** Null start date. Source: `data/seed/texts.json`.
- **coordinateWorks:** Finite latitude and longitude; presence does not establish geographic precision. Source: `data/seed/texts.json`.
- **unlocatedWorks:** Missing either coordinate. Source: `data/seed/texts.json`.
- **mappedWorks:** Shared map predicate includes undated coordinate-bearing records by default, all traditions; unknown dates are not placed in time. Source: `packages/shared/exploration.ts + data/seed/texts.json`.
- **timelineWorks:** Current dated-through-year predicate, all traditions. Source: `packages/shared/exploration.ts + data/seed/texts.json`.
- **languageMetadataWorks:** Language labels only, not original-language transcriptions. Source: `data/seed/texts.json`.

## Limitations

- Source presence is not scholarly review or completion.
- Image rights are retained intake metadata, not live URL/license re-verification.
- Zero absent-dataset counts are limited to the reviewed seed registry; new source files require adapter reconciliation.
- No production/private notes or credentials are read or published.
