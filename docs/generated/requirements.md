## Quality and regression rules

All rules remain unverified until actual requirement-level evidence is attached. Existing passing tests do not automatically close these rules.

- **EXP-01 — unverified:** Undeciphered texts never receive generated translations.
- **EXP-02 — unverified:** Lost texts never receive fabricated complete passages.
- **EXP-03 — unverified:** Hypothetical sources never receive fabricated quotations.
- **EXP-04 — unverified:** Copyrighted works are never fully ingested without permission.
- **EXP-05 — unverified:** Every image has rights and provenance.
- **EXP-06 — unverified:** Modern Anunnaki claims never appear as ancient primary evidence.
- **EXP-07 — unverified:** Codex Gigas is a manuscript compilation, not scripture.
- **EXP-08 — unverified:** Inferno and Faust are literary works, not scripture.
- **EXP-09 — unverified:** Scientology is categorized as a modern religious tradition.
- **EXP-10 — unverified:** Ethiopian Meqabyan is not automatically equated with Greek Maccabees.
- **EXP-11 — unverified:** Ethiopian canon is modeled by tradition/edition and not flattened into Western enumeration.
- **EXP-12 — unverified:** Indigenous oral traditions are not automatically called scripture.
- **EXP-13 — unverified:** Restricted Indigenous material cannot be bulk exposed.
- **EXP-14 — unverified:** Colonial transcriptions identify their recorder and context.
- **EXP-15 — unverified:** Disputed authenticity is visible prominently.
- **EXP-16 — unverified:** AI-generated translation is visibly marked as AI-generated.
- **EXP-17 — unverified:** Primary sources outrank modern popular claims in Scholarly search mode.
- **EXP-18 — unverified:** Broad search can still surface alternative/speculative material with evidence labels.
- **EXP-19 — unverified:** “Banned” requires who, where, when, and why.
- **EXP-20 — unverified:** Canon exclusion is not classified automatically as suppression.
- **EXP-21 — unverified:** Similarity does not automatically generate an influence edge.
- **EXP-22 — unverified:** Every historical relationship has evidence and confidence.
- **EXP-23 — unverified:** Existing tests continue to pass.
- **HIST-01 — unverified:** Political borders and religious influence use separate map layers.
- **HIST-02 — unverified:** Government religion is not treated as population religion.
- **HIST-03 — unverified:** Pre-conflict/post-conflict map states remain independent.
- **HIST-04 — unverified:** Unknown historical demographics remain unknown.
- **HIST-05 — unverified:** Approximate boundaries are labeled approximate.
- **HIST-06 — unverified:** Disputed boundaries remain disputed.
- **HIST-07 — unverified:** A conflict can have multiple causes.
- **HIST-08 — unverified:** Religious participants do not automatically make a conflict primarily religious.
- **HIST-09 — unverified:** Forced-conversion classification requires evidence.
