# Corpus review roadmap

The complete specification list is present as 248 distinct metadata records after duplicate removal. Four additional works bring the catalog to 252. Searchable sources cover 43 works, including 41 complete named works or editions and two historical studies with selections. Other records remain metadata-only.

Next editorial priorities are licensed and source-checked editions from Mesopotamia, Egypt, Africa, Mesoamerica, Oceania, Jainism and Sikhism; original-language and manuscript datasets; community-specific canon histories; and scholarly review of provisional dates and place anchors. Indigenous and oral materials require a culturally appropriate selection and permissions process. These priorities are dataset work, not automatically generated substitute history.

Every added edition should pass rights review, exact source/translator identification, source-word preservation tests, deterministic segmentation, corpus indexing and UI citation inspection. New historical relationships need independent evidence for chronology, contact and specificity; motif overlap alone is insufficient.
